## Enunciado

$X\sim\text{Poisson}(\lambda)$. Mostre que $P(X\ge 2\lambda)\le\left(\dfrac{e}{4}\right)^\lambda$.

## Passo 1: FGM de Poisson

$$\phi_X(t)=e^{\lambda(e^t-1)}.$$

## Passo 2: Limitante de Chernoff (Q3) com $x_0=2\lambda$

$$P(X\ge 2\lambda)\le\frac{\phi_X(t)}{e^{2\lambda t}}=\exp\!\left\{\lambda(e^t-1)-2\lambda t\right\}=\exp\!\left\{\lambda(e^t-2t-1)\right\}.$$

## Passo 3: Minimização em $t$

Seja $g(t)=e^t-2t-1$. Derivando: $g'(t)=e^t-2=0\Rightarrow t^*=\ln 2$.

$$g(t^*)=2-2\ln 2-1=1-\ln 4=\ln(e/4).$$

$$P(X\ge 2\lambda)\le e^{\lambda\ln(e/4)}=\left(\frac{e}{4}\right)^\lambda. \quad\blacksquare$$
