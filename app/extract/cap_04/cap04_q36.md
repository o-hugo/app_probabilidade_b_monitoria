## Enunciado

Sejam $X$, $Y$ e $Z$ variáveis aleatórias independentes com a mesma distribuição geométrica de parâmetro $p$ ($P[X=j]=(1-p)^j p$, $j \geq 0$). Determine:

**(a)** $P(X = Y)$. **(b)** $P(X \ge 2Y)$. **(c)** $P(X + Y \le Z)$.

## Passo 1: Item (a) — $P(X=Y)$

$$P(X=Y) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} P[X=k]P[Y=k] = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} p^2(1-p)^{2k} = \frac{p^2}{1-(1-p)^2} = \frac{p^2}{p(2-p)} = \frac{p}{2-p}.$$

**Resumo:** $P(X=Y) = p/(2-p)$.

## Passo 2: Item (b) — $P(X \ge 2Y)$

$$P(X \ge 2Y) = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} P[Y=j]\,P[X \ge 2j] = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} p(1-p)^j \cdot (1-p)^{2j} = p \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(1-p)^{3j} = \frac{p}{1-(1-p)^3}.$$

**Resumo:** $P(X \ge 2Y) = p/[1-(1-p)^3]$.

## Passo 3: Item (c) — $P(X+Y \le Z)$

De Q31, $P[X+Y=n] = (n+1)p^2(1-p)^n$. Além disso, $P[Z \ge n] = (1-p)^n$.

$$P(X+Y \le Z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} P[X+Y=n]\,P[Z \ge n] = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(n+1)p^2(1-p)^n(1-p)^n$$

$$= p^2 \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(n+1)(1-p)^{2n} = \frac{p^2}{[1-(1-p)^2]^2} = \frac{p^2}{p^2(2-p)^2} = \frac{1}{(2-p)^2}.$$

**Resumo:** $P(X+Y \le Z) = 1/(2-p)^2$.
